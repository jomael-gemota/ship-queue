## Welcome to Ship Queue

**Ship Queue** is the internal tool your team uses to turn incoming orders into
printed shipping labels — quickly and in bulk.

You don't need any technical knowledge to use it. If you can fill out a
spreadsheet and click a few buttons, you can use Ship Queue.

![The Ship Queue Orders page](/screenshots/orders.png)

### What can it do?

- **Bring in orders automatically** from ShipStation, so your list is always up to date.
- **Group orders into a batch** from a simple spreadsheet you upload.
- **Create and print shipping labels** for the whole batch at once.
- **Save label PDFs to Google Drive** automatically.
- **Pull shareable file links** from Dropbox with the Dropbox Fetcher.

### New here?

Start with **[Signing In & Getting Around](/guide/getting-started)**. It takes
about two minutes to read and shows you where everything is.

::: tip A note on access
Some actions (like creating labels or managing users) depend on the permissions
your admin has given you. If a button is missing or greyed out, see
[FAQ & Troubleshooting](/guide/faq) or ask an admin.
:::
