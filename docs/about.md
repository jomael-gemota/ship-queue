## What is Ship Queue?

Ship Queue is an internal bulk-shipping tool for our fulfillment team. It
connects to **ShipStation**, pulls in orders automatically, and lets you create
and print carrier shipping labels in batches — then saves the label PDFs to
Google Drive. The goal is simple: take the repetitive, error-prone parts of
label creation and make them fast, consistent, and easy for anyone on the team.

## Why it was built

Creating shipping labels one at a time is slow and easy to get wrong, especially
during busy periods. Ship Queue was built to:

- Keep the order list current without manual effort (automatic syncing).
- Turn a simple spreadsheet into a reviewed batch of labels.
- Create and print many labels at once, with the PDFs archived to Google Drive.
- Keep access controlled, so only the right people can create labels or manage
  users.
