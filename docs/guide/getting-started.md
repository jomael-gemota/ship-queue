# Signing In & Getting Around

This page shows you how to sign in and find your way around Ship Queue. It takes
about two minutes.

## Signing in

Ship Queue uses your **company Google account** to sign in — there is no
separate password to remember.

1. Open Ship Queue in your web browser.
2. On the welcome screen, click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose your company Google account and approve access.
4. You'll land on the **Orders** screen. You're in!

![The Ship Queue sign-in screen with the "Continue with Google" button](/screenshots/login.png)

::: warning Who can sign in
Access is limited to approved company accounts (for example
`@outdoorequipped.com` and `@channelprecision.com`). If you see an "Access
denied" message, you're signing in with the wrong account — switch to your
company account and try again.
:::

## The layout

Once you're signed in, the screen has three main parts:

- **Sidebar (left)** — your main menu. Use it to move between pages like Orders,
  Create Shipping Label, Dropbox Fetcher, Settings, and User Management.
- **Header (top)** — shows the name of the page you're on, today's date, a
  **Guide** link (it opens this guide), and a light/dark mode switch.
- **Main area (center)** — the page you're currently working on.

![The top header bar showing the page title, date, Guide link, and dark mode switch](/screenshots/header.png)

_The header — note the **Guide** button, which opens this guide in a new tab._

### The menu, section by section

![The sidebar menu with Operations and Admin sections](/screenshots/sidebar.png)

The sidebar is split into two groups:

**Operations**

| Menu item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **ShipStation Orders** | Browse the orders pulled in from ShipStation. |
| **Create Shipping Label** | Upload a spreadsheet and make shipping labels. |
| **Dropbox Fetcher** | Grab shareable links for files in Dropbox. |

**Admin**

| Menu item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings** | Connect Google Drive / Dropbox and set sync options. |
| **User Management** | (Admins) Manage people and their access. |

::: tip Everyone sees the full menu
You can open every page in the menu. Some pages may show a short note if you
don't have permission for a particular action — that's normal.
:::

## Switching light and dark mode

In the top-right corner, click the **Light / Dark Mode** button to switch
between a bright theme and a darker one. Pick whichever is easier on your eyes —
your choice is remembered.

## Opening this guide anytime

The **Guide** link in the header (top of every page) opens this guide in a new
browser tab, so you never lose your place in the app.

## Signing out

At the bottom of the sidebar, click **Sign out** when you're done.
